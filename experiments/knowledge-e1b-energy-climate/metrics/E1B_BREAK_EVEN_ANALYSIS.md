# E1B break-even analysis

The metric is a **semantic-interpretation proxy**, not elapsed time, token cost, or money. Direct costs 12 interpretation obligations per derivative in the K1/K2 overlap profile. Knowledge first-use proxy is 12 curation + 6 references = 18. Under the observed-medium profile, each additional derivative adds 5 new obligations + 6.5 references = 11.5, giving a projected break-even around **N=13**. High reuse projects N=3; low reuse does not break even through N=20.

Observed four-kit corpus: no break-even yet. The result therefore supports an **optional** Knowledge path for repeated/evolving overlapping derivatives, not universal adoption.
